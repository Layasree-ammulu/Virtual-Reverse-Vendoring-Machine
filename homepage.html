Plastic Waste Awareness

Plastic pollution has reached a critical level, with over 400 million tons of plastic produced annually, half of which is single-use. Only 9% of plastic waste is recycled, while the majority ends up in landfills, oceans, or the environment. Each year, at least 14 million tons of plastic enter the ocean, contributing to massive pollution, including the Great Pacific Garbage Patch. Microplastics are now found everywhere— from deep-sea trenches to human bodies—posing potential health risks.
