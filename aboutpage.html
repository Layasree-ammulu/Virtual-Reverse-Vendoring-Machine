What is VRVM?

The Virtual Reverse Vending Machine (VRVM) is a digital alternative to traditional reverse vending machines (RVMs), designed to encourage recycling through mobile apps and digital tracking. Unlike traditional RVMs—which accept used beverage containers and provide rewards—VRVMs leverage mobile apps, QR codes, and digital tracking to encourage recycling.

Why Use VRVM?

  • Encourages sustainable waste management.
  • Earn rewards while keeping the planet clean.
  • Seamless and user-friendly experience.
