How to Recycle with Eco Plast

Step 1: Create an account or log in to get started.

Step 2: Click on "Start Recycling" and select the plastic item.

Step 3: Insert the plastic item into the machine. It will scan and verify.

Step 4: Earn points according to the type of plastic disposed and redeem them for rewards.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What types of plastic can I recycle?

A: You can recycle PET bottles, HDPE containers, plastic bags, and plastic cups.

Q: How do I redeem my rewards?

A: Once you earn enough points, go to the Rewards section and select your redemption option.

Q: What happens if the machine doesn't accept my item?

A: Ensure the item is clean and not damaged. If the issue persists, contact support.
